# Greedy & Interval Scheduling

Optimal greedy algorithms for compatible resource and interval selection.

---

## 1. Activity Selection Problem
- Greedily selects activities with the earliest finish time to maximize remaining compatible slots.
- **Time Complexity:** $O(N \log N)$
- **Space Complexity:** $O(1)$
