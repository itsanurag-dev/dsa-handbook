## Highlights

- **Self-Balancing Trees:** AVL Tree supporting LL, RR, LR, RL rotations and $O(\log N)$ deletion.
- **Advanced Caches:** $O(1)$ LFU Cache with LRU tie-breaking and Separate Chaining Hash Table.
- **Range Queries:** Segment Tree with Lazy Propagation and Fenwick Tree (Binary Indexed Tree).
- **Graph Algorithms:** Kruskal & Prim MST, Bellman-Ford, Floyd-Warshall, and Tarjan's Bridges/SCC.
- **String Algorithms:** Knuth-Morris-Pratt (KMP) and Rabin-Karp rolling polynomial hashing.
